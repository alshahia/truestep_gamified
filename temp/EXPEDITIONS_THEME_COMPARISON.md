## 2. Core Differences by Feature

### A. Narrative & Progression Fantasy
- **Option A (Solo Leveling):** You are an "awakened" hunter whose real-world walking breaks open dimensional portals. Walking 5 km clears an E-Rank Goblin Den; walking 25 km breaches the Jeju Island S-Rank Red Gate. Rewards are Mana Crystals, Runestones, and Hunter Stat Points (STR, AGI, VIT, SEN).
- **Option B (Cyberpunk 2077):** You are a mercenary/runner operating across Night City districts. Walking clears mercenary gigs, raises your Street Cred, and unlocks black-market cyberware mods from Ripperdocs.
- **Option C (Hybrid Arcade):** Combines the dimensional gates and stat leveling of Solo Leveling with the neon chrome aesthetic and arcade combo adrenaline of Cyberpunk 2077. High-cadence walking activates "Sandevistan Overdrive" or "Mana Surge" with arcade score multipliers.

### B. UI Component Architecture & Visual Tokens
- **Option A:** Uses the iconic **Translucent Cyan/Blue System Window** with sharp technical corners, glowing borders (`#00F0FF`), and glowing gate portals with swirling particle effects.
- **Option B:** Uses angled chamfered cards, vibrant **Neon Yellow (`#FCEE09`) and Cyber Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**, glitch text accents, and district city skyline backdrops.
- **Option C:** Blends **Hyper Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**, **Electric Mint (`#4DFFB2`)**, and **Neon Violet (`#7B61FF`)** with pixel arcade badges, live combo meters, and boss encounter alerts.

### C. Impact on Milestone Achievements
- **Option A:** "E-Rank Awakening", "Shadow Monarch Trial", "Iron Will Stride", "Demon Castle Conqueror".
- **Option B:** "Street Samurai", "Netrunner Overclock", "Night City Legend", "Arasaka Tower Infiltrator".
- **Option C:** "Cybernetic Awakening", "Apex Hunter Grade S", "Chrono Overdrive Master", "Glitch Breaker".
