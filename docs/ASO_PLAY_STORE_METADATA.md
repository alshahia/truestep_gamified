## 3. Data Safety & Health Declarations

### Privacy Policy
* **In-App Policy:** Integrated directly in Settings > *Privacy Policy & Data Rights*.
* **Hosted URL:** Required for Google Play Console submission.
* **Data Collected:** None. All data is stored locally in device Room DB.
* **Data Shared:** None. Zero third-party analytics or ad SDKs.
* **Health Connect Declaration:** 
  * Permission: `android.permission.health.READ_STEPS`
  * Justification: To read and reconcile step records from wearables onto the user's local dashboard.
  * Write Operations: None (TrueStep never writes to Health Connect in MVP).

## 6. Post-Launch ASO Tactics

1. **Google Play In-App Review Flow:**
   * Trigger the Google Play In-App Review API only at high-satisfaction milestones:
     * When the user achieves their 7-day goal streak.
     * When earning a Streak Freeze in the Streak Vault.
     * When unlocking a milestone trophy.
   * *Never* interrupt onboarding or first-time launch.
2. **Store Listing Experiments (A/B Testing):**
   * Run Google Play Console experiments testing Feature Graphic variants (Minimalist dark vs. high-contrast teal).
   * Test Title Option A (`TrueStep: Pedometer & Steps`) against Title Option B (`TrueStep: Offline Pedometer`).
3. **Android Vitals Maintenance:**
   * Keep Crash rate strictly below 0.5% (Google bad behavior threshold is 1.09%).
   * Keep ANR rate strictly below 0.2% (Google bad behavior threshold is 0.47%).
   * Batched sensor updates and foreground service health type prevent background battery warnings.
